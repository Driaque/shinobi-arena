Both assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool, inspected, copied into the repository and encoded to WebP without resizing. The delivered atlas is 1254 × 1254 RGBA; the arena is 1672 × 941. Generated output dimensions differed from the requested dimensions; runtime texture views use the actual dimensions.

Final runtime paths:

- `apps/web/assets/visual-lab/aren-atlas.webp`

## Aren atlas — transparent background

Use case: stylized-concept. Production asset for Fabled Rivals, an original 2D side-view fighting game. Create a single transparent PNG sprite atlas, square 2048 by 2048, EXACT 4 columns by 4 rows of equally sized cells, exactly sixteen full-body poses of ONE identical ORIGINAL adult male fighter, Aren, the Gale Vanguard. Athletic dark-skinned young adult, short ivory-white locs tied back, teal long sleeveless split coat over fitted charcoal combat clothing, warm brass armor details, wrapped hands, sturdy boots, a short curved single-edged sword. Original fantasy martial artist, no existing anime character, no ninja headband, no copyrighted insignia. Polished hand-painted anime game sprites with crisp ink contour, cel shading and detailed cloth/armor. All sixteen figures face RIGHT in 3/4 side view; consistent body proportions, identical costume, feet at same baseline within each cell except airborne poses. Each full figure occupies central 65 percent of its cell with generous transparent margins, nothing crosses cell edges. No words, labels, grid lines, or visible cell boundaries. Row 1: relaxed combat idle; breathing idle; running right with left leg forward; running right with right leg forward. Row 2: low forward dash; jump launch with bent knees; airborne rising with sword drawn; descending landing with bent knees. Row 3: sword attack windup; horizontal sword slash; sword slash followthrough; overhead aerial sword strike. Row 4: crouched guard; recoil from hit; open strong awakening pose with subtle teal energy; poised elemental palm thrust. Perfectly clean transparent background, actual alpha, no checkerboard. Not a character poster: this is a regular 4x4 sprite sheet for direct game use.

## Skyglass Terrace — opaque background

Use case: stylized-concept. Production background asset for Fabled Rivals, original 2D side-view arena fighting game. Wide 16:9 landscape, 2048x1152 if possible. An exquisitely detailed hand-painted fantasy arena called The Skyglass Terrace: huge flat ancient pale blue-grey stone dueling terrace in the lower third, broad clear empty fighting space, terrace floor begins at exactly 72 percent of image height and stretches all the way to bottom. Beyond it a sprawling mountain citadel of original angular brass and weathered stone architecture, suspended stone bridges, cascading waterfalls, cloud-filled chasms and distant layered mountains at blue hour. Huge glowing crescent moon and soft peach sunset on horizon, teal magical lanterns, windblown hanging pennants at far edges only. Rich atmospheric perspective and delicate environmental detail, beautiful painted anime fighting game scenery, sophisticated blue/teal and amber palette, clear readable silhouettes and clean open central play area. Side-on game camera, no tilted perspective, no characters, no people, no creatures, no UI, no text, no logo, no recognizable existing anime buildings or insignia. Keep near foreground free of large obstacles so fighters remain readable.
