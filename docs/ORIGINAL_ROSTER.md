# Fabled Rivals: The Broken Accord

Skyglass Terrace once connected the river courts, the northern pass and the suspended districts. The theft of its Heart shattered that accord. Each rival holds a different piece of the truth: a witness ledger, an evacuation record, a changed design or an order they regret obeying. Their alliances do not require the same ending: restoration, justice and control are competing aims.

## Silent — Cold Metal

Once the keeper of Hyorin’s northern pass, Silent sealed a fractured Skyglass conduit inside his own armor to stop a catastrophe. He now searches for its missing heart before the seal destroys his home.

Dora is his wife and closest strategist. His blood brother Winta held the pass beside him. Rook forged the armor that keeps him alive.

Play style: Precision sword counters. Tetsugan reflects guarded hits; Echoing Rift pulls and exposes metal vulnerability.

## Winta — Frozen Sun

Winta survived the northern breach by turning a failing conduit into a crimson storm. Celebrated as a rescuer, he carries the guilt of abandoning a rescue order that could have saved Oryn’s expedition.

Silent’s blood brother and trusted battle companion. Oryn blames him for the expedition’s loss. Eira alone has read his unaltered rescue report.

Play style: Lightning sword pressure, dash attacks and recovery through clean drain hits. Shinrai Ōu strengthens lightning.

## Dora — AquaPrincess

Dora, Silent’s wife, is Hyorin’s strongest kunoichi and the royal envoy who united the river courts. When the Terrace fell, she chose to shelter its refugees instead of surrendering them to the council. She fights to rebuild the alliance on her own terms.

Silent’s wife. Veyra was once her council adviser. Nyra owes her sanctuary; Aren carries her messages between the broken cities.

Play style: Graceful wind, water and fire counterattacks. Weaken, pull and punish with bladed fans; Limit Release amplifies wind and fire.

## Aren — Gale Vanguard

A courier from the suspended districts, Aren witnessed the theft of the Skyglass Heart. Branded a thief after refusing to hand over the witness ledger, he learned to fight across collapsing bridges while carrying the evidence home.

Eira is his older sister. Dora protects his route. Kael wants the ledger; Nyra knows the secret passage he used to escape.

Play style: Launch with Rising Gale, chase into aerial sword combos and confirm with Sky Cleave. Equip Diving Flame for a finisher.

## Eira — Tide Archivist

The last archivist of the Terrace, Eira can read memories held in flowing water. She preserved the final evacuation record, including proof that the council ordered the northern gates shut before the alarms sounded.

Aren’s older sister. Shares the rescue record with Winta. Veyra needs her testimony silenced; Oryn needs it to learn what happened.

Play style: Water field control and ranged senbon. Manage lanes and punish opponents committed to crossing flooded ground.

## Kael — Ashbound Marshal

Kael commanded the evacuation and obeyed the order to close the gates. Convinced the missing Heart will ignite another disaster, he pursues Aren to recover it, even as his faith in the council begins to fracture.

Former mentor to Rook. Pursues Aren’s ledger. Veyra issued the order he followed; Silent refuses to forgive its cost.

Play style: Fire terrain pressure with an aerial finisher. Hold space with Ember Field and punish jumps with deliberate sword attacks.

## Nyra — Thorn Apothecary

Nyra treated conduit sickness in the districts the council abandoned. Accused of spreading the illness she was studying, she escaped through the maintenance tunnels and now hunts the source of the contamination.

Dora granted her sanctuary. Guided Aren out of the Terrace. Supplies Oryn with medicine while questioning the bargain that sustains him.

Play style: Poison projectiles, lingering mist and patient spacing. Threaten approaches, then switch weapons when enemies close in.

## Rook — Iron Oath

Rook built the Terrace’s emergency armor and discovered its safety valves had been removed. He forged Silent’s seal from the wreckage, then abandoned his marshal’s commission to find who altered his designs.

Kael’s former apprentice. Forged Silent’s armor. Eira holds his signed plans, while Veyra holds the orders that changed them.

Play style: Earth binds and defensive wards. Create a safe approach, bait a committed strike and answer with a heavy weapon.

## Veyra — Thread Regent

Once Dora’s adviser, Veyra believes the cities will tear one another apart without a single ruler. She ordered the Heart removed to end the council’s dependence on it, then concealed the cost of the extraction.

Dora’s former adviser and political rival. Gave Kael the gate order. Seeks Eira’s archive and Aren’s ledger before the truth unites her opponents.

Play style: Binding pressure and wind defense. Restrict movement, force a predictable escape and counter from mid-range.

## Oryn — Hollow Lantern

Oryn led the repair expedition trapped beneath the Terrace. A damaged lantern engine kept him alive by drawing energy from its surroundings. He returned searching for his lost crew, unsure how much of himself the engine has consumed.

Blames Winta for the abandoned rescue. Nyra keeps his condition stable. Eira’s recovered record may redirect his anger toward the council.

Play style: Resource drain and calculated recovery. Exhaust an opponent’s options, then turn clean hits into renewed pressure.
