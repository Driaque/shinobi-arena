# The Northern Calamity — Fabled Rivals originals

Silent and Winta are original Fabled Rivals characters and blood brothers from Frostmere. Their abilities and shared history belong to the Skyglass setting. This document describes their selected playable kits and separates future concepts from current mechanics.

## Silent — Cold Metal

Frostmere; Skyforged Aegis bloodline. Light skin, long silver-grey hair, ice-blue eyes, dark face mask, Frostmere crest headband and navy/silver/black northern shinobi armor. Frostmere Saber-style sword and Oathknife dagger. Elements: Metal, Water, Earth, Fire, Wind and Lightning.

Established ability pool: Middle Guard Stance, Harmonious Slash, Equilibrium Guard Strike, Vacuum Fan, Phantom Step, Dragon King's Impact, Blazing Fang Strike, Chakra Resonance, Echoing Rift, Stone of the Withering, Rimeflash, Abyssal Surge.

Archetype: precision counterattacker. Three metal sword basics replace the generic unarmed chain. Frostmere Saber and Oathknife are selectable before battle; selecting another equipped weapon does not change the built-in sword basics.

| Input (default P1) | Technique | First playable rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J ×3 | Opening Cut → Returning Edge → Rising Steel | Sword combo, standard confirm/cancel and scaling rules; 182 total damage without modifiers |
| U | Harmonious Slash | 125 damage, 25 chakra, 165 reach, 50% guard penetration |
| I | Echoing Rift | 70 damage, 30 chakra, 270 reach; clean hit pulls up to 90 units and applies +20% incoming **metal** damage for 3 seconds |

A successful guarded hit readies one +35% sword counter for 1.5 seconds; the next sword contact consumes it, even against guard. Partial pierce reduces reflect; full pierce bypasses the stance. |

Metal vulnerability is a damage modifier, never periodic damage. Rift does not pull/apply vulnerability through ordinary guard or Replacement. Guard stance is the initial defensive stance; offensive stance switching, additional moves and penguin companions remain future work.

## Winta — Frozen Sun

Frostmere storm sentinel; Stormheart bloodline. Profile describes long dark hair, crimson/maroon northern samurai armor and an optional kasa, with crimson lightning, embers and snow. The supplied black/gold lightning portrait is the temporary visual concept. The stage fighter currently combines dark hair, maroon armor, gold eyes and crimson lightning. Sword weapon. Elements: Lightning, Water and Fire.

Associated ability pool: Rimeflash, Inner Peace, Harmonious Slash, Chakra Overflow, Eclipse Drain, Chakra Resonance.

Archetype: elemental pressure fighter. Three lightning sword basics use the usual confirm and scaling system.

| Input (default P1) | Technique | First playable rules |
| --- | --- | --- |

| U | Rimeflash | 125 lightning damage, 25 chakra; forward dash during active frames, no invulnerability |

| O | Stormheart | 30 chakra; +15% lightning damage for 5 seconds, 10-second cooldown. Does not enhance water or equipped metal weapons. |
| T | Frozen Sun Dominion (**proposed**) | Unlock after 400 cumulative actual HP lost, once per round; 10 seconds. +15% outgoing damage, +20% movement/dash speed, 5% less incoming damage, 25% faster basic-chain animation progress. Recovery in the first kit comes from Eclipse Drain. Inner Peace and other techniques are not yet bound. Ability names, lore and real-time damage/effect mappings belong to the Fabled Rivals character design.

## Shared behavior and future duo

Both are available for P1, local P2 and the CPU under Fabled Rivals Originals. Portraits use the supplied concept images; stage fighters remain procedural, not finished sprites. Mobile analog, double jump/dash, Replacement and customizable saved controls work as before. Weapons remain freely selectable for all characters.

Duo: **The Northern Calamity — Frozen Sun & Cold Metal**. **Northern Calamity: Frozen Metal Storm** remains a proposed team ultimate. It is not exposed as a working action because the game currently has two solo fighters, no team/assist mode.

Future kit expansion can add stance switching, explicit stun techniques and further elemental interactions after these mechanics have been playtested. Neither the longer ability pool nor the duo ultimate is advertised as already playable.

## Dora — AquaPrincess (v0.14.0)

Dora joins Fabled Rivals Originals as a selectable wind/water/fire counterattacker. Silent’s wife; bladed Tideglass Fans and Skyforged Blade. Water weakens, wind pulls into a defendable fire finisher, Triune Flow rewards guarded hits, and damage unlocks Limit Release. Her generated regal portrait follows the user’s appearance corrections.
