# Dora — AquaPrincess

User-created original for Shinobi Arena: Silent’s wife, a graceful, smart, strong heroine with wind, water and fire. Described as the strongest kunoichi in her story; lore strength does not bypass shared combat rules.

## Appearance

Light golden-brown skin, brown eyes, profile height 5′7″. Regal aqua/navy robes, gold detailing, necklace and earrings. Long dark hair is an initial concept choice because that field was left open. The portrait is fictional, not a likeness of the user’s wife. Normal-form generated portrait and procedural stage graphics are shipped; no detailed animation sprites or awakening portrait are bundled.

## Play style and inputs

Calculated ranged setups and melee counters. Selecting Dora suggests fans and katana, both editable. Consumables retain four slots. All moves obey normal stamina, chakra, guard, hit-confirm, cooldown and damage-scaling rules.

| P1 default | Technique | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J ×3 | Rippling Fan → Crossing Breeze → Royal Ascent | Metal/wind fan chain, 182 total scaled damage before modifiers |
| H | Equipped weapon | Fans: 85 damage, 145 reach, 16 stamina. F swaps to other weapon |
| U | Tidal Edict | 65 water damage, 25 chakra, 210-tick cooldown; clean hit: 25% weaken for 180 ticks |
| I | Royal Tempest | 35 wind damage, 280 reach, 40 chakra, 360-tick cooldown, pull up to 150, 20% weaken for 150 ticks; clean hit starts Crown Breaker: 130 fire damage, 170 reach, 18-tick startup, 32-tick recovery |
| O | Triune Flow | 30 chakra, 180-tick stance, 480-tick cooldown. A guarded non-full-pierce hit readies one 35% fan/sword counter for 90 ticks; no reflect or absorb |
| T | Limit Release | 400 cumulative damage unlock, 600 ticks, once per round; +10% damage/movement, 15% faster basic chain. Royal Gale burns; Sovereign Vortex pulls farther into Blazing Crown |

Weaken refreshes to the stronger potency and longer remaining duration rather than multiplying. Royal Tempest never pulls through ordinary guard or invulnerability. Its automatic heavy follow-up commits Dora to a separate attack; it costs no additional chakra, can be interrupted and cannot basic-chain cancel. The opener’s hitstun is 12 ticks, leaving six ticks before the follow-up becomes active for guard, parry, movement or Replacement. Full pierce, low stamina, cooldowns and missed-attack recovery constrain her pressure.

Limit Release extends her procedural hair and subtly widens the flowing robe silhouette, with an aqua wind aura. Her stronger fire techniques use warm effects. The supplied awakening name and victory catchphrase “Yaaay” are retained; move names and numerical balance are proposed game adaptations, not established TNR canon.
