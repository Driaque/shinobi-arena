# Fabled Rivals character kits

The ten core rivals use the Skyglass world, Frostmere, the river courts and conduit technology. Twenty-five additional original rivals are marked unreleased and are visible only with Include unreleased rivals enabled.

The unreleased cast has new names, silhouettes, palettes, histories and faction relationships. Their elite presentation does not grant higher competitive HP or damage. See packages/engine/future-fighters.js for current descriptions and kits.

Techniques, awakening forms, weapons and consumables use original-world names. Existing equipment limits, costs, cooldowns and combat rules remain applicable. One-way identifier fingerprints migrate compatible saved loadouts without exposing superseded content names.
