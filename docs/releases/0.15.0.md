# Shinobi Arena v0.15.0

## Strategy loadouts

- Choose from a 54-jutsu grid, with costs, range, cooldowns, effects and fighter-specific bloodline restrictions. Other techniques can be cross-trained as gameplay adaptations.
- Every match allows up to seven distinct jutsus, two weapons and four consumables. At least one weapon is required; other slots may be empty. Both clients and the authoritative server validate loadouts.
- Weapons and consumables now use grid cards. Choose an equipment slot, then a card, and Save. Cancel discards changes. Saved loadouts are remembered per fighter on this browser.
- U / I / O cast slots 1–3; 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 cast slots 4–7. Mobile: open Jutsus to access all seven equipped techniques. The three primary touch buttons show the currently equipped techniques. P2 extra slots use Num 1–4.
- Hold G to charge chakra (P2 Num 0), or hold Charge inside the Jutsus palette. Charging is stationary, consumes stamina and creates a punish opening.

## Combat effects

- Elemental ground fields: Ember Field burns, Flood Field slows, Stone Snare binds, Venom Mist applies poison-slow. Fields last four seconds, target enemies near the ground and can be escaped by leaving their radius or jumping before a bind lands. Two fields per fighter maximum.
- Poison-slow damages over time and reduces movement; bind stops movement, jumps and dashes while guard, attacks and Replacement remain available. Bleed, timed haste, protective ward, cleansing, healing and strong knockback expand the strategy library.
- Water Absorption requires front guard and converts ordinary enemy projectiles into chakra. Bombs, full pierce and melee counter it. Chakra-steal attacks transfer only available chakra and never exceed 100.
- Winta’s Mizukumi retains its active dash and now steals chakra on clean hits. Eclipse Drain retains clean-hit lifesteal and also steals chakra. Guard prevents these gains.

## Online arena

- Arena → Rankings shows server-validated Elo ratings, five-game placements and rank tiers. Casual and ranked queues are separate. A private recovery code restores the same profile on another browser; display names alone do not establish identity.
- Completed matches and forfeits are saved idempotently. Casual fights do not change Elo. The first three ranked matches per opponent per UTC day affect rating. Winner rewards require 20 seconds of active play; early forfeits penalize the leaver without rewarding the winner. These are prototype anti-farming rules, not verified-person accounts.
- Arena → Live matches lists discoverable fights. Spectators receive read-only snapshots, occupy no player slot and cannot send combat or ready actions. Public rooms have a 40-spectator cap.
- Arena → Past fights provides archived replay playback, pause, seek, 0.5× / 1× / 2× speed and download. Replays contain recorded snapshots, accepted inputs, round transitions and the original catalog so later balance changes do not rewrite the fight. Replaying does not join a live room or write a result.
- Replay retention is 14 days and the latest 200 archives. Match results and ratings outlive replay retention. Recordings are bounded; any recording limit is explicitly labeled.

## Compatibility and hosting

- Refresh every device. Protocol 3 / rules duel-0.15.0 require compatible client and server versions.
- Controls migrate to settings version 6 while preserving custom bindings and positions; conflicting new bindings remain unbound until configured.
- Durable online data uses PostgreSQL. Local development supports a persistent SQLite file; an in-memory preview is clearly labeled and cannot run ranked matches.
- The Render preview database expires November 27, 2026. Export or migrate it before expiry to retain profiles, ratings and history. The expiry is also shown in Arena.
- Character art remains procedural in battle. Detailed sprite animation, verified-person accounts, reconnect/rollback networking and private rooms are future work.
