# Fabled Rivals v0.42.0

## Mobile touch

Expanded touch is the default on touch devices with fresh control settings. The battlefield extends behind the controls in portrait and landscape. Movement sits on the left with jump, attack, guard, and dashes on the right. Left-handed mode mirrors those positions. The combat camera raises the ground line to give the action room above the buttons.

Open Kit to use weapons, consumables, jutsus, Replacement, and awakening. The button shows remaining items and ready jutsus. Under Settings → Controls → Placement, choose Expanded touch, Below arena, or the customizable Over arena preset. Previously saved layouts load as before.

## Roster and art

All 35 slots are visible and selectable. The 24 fighters awaiting combat sprites appear shaded on the roster and currently use procedural bodies in battle. Vael joins the ten original fighters with a painted combat atlas. The WebGL and Canvas renderers use a painted background while the selected arena art loads.

## Compatibility and limits

Saved controls, loadouts, online rules, and arena movement bounds are compatible with v0.41.1. Expanded touch changes the visible viewport and camera framing. Browser coverage includes portrait and landscape on Chromium. Physical mobile testing follows deployment.

## Planned

Preview fighter unlocks will be tied to their Story encounters as the chapters arrive. All preview fighters are selectable in this mobile test release. New stage paintings and the remaining fighter atlases are future art work.
