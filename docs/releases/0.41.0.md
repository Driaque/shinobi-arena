# Fabled Rivals v0.41.0

## Fight feel

- A dynamic fight camera closes in on exchanges and pulls back when fighters separate, keeping heads and the floor in frame. It never shows more than the normal view.
- Landed hits pause briefly, heavier blows hold longer and the finishing blow is held before the result appears. Hits also shake the screen slightly, punch the zoom and flash the struck fighter.
- Both can be switched off under Feel: "Dynamic fight camera" and "Impact pause on hits". Reduced motion keeps the camera fixed and drops shake and flashes.
- Impact pause applies to offline modes only. Online and co-op timing are unchanged.

## Sprites only for released fighters

- The ten released rivals always fight as painted sprites; the fighter-style selector is gone. The procedural body remains only as a fallback when a sheet fails to load, and for the twenty-five unreleased preview rivals, which have no sprite sheets yet.
- Weapon attacks draw the painted weapon in the fighter's leading hand with a slash trail, replacing line-drawn weapons. Unarmed strikes get a tapered swoosh and the Orbit Disc flies as its painted disc.
- Three damaged PNG exports (Skyglass and Forge stage sizes and one Rook portrait size) are repaired.

## The Rival's Crest, Mark and items

- Guarding raises five plates in the fighter's own colour. Each plate is a fifth of the guard meter and they fail from the outside in. The rim burns gold while a parry is still possible, a blocked blow runs along the rim, a parry answers with a riposte seal and a broken guard cracks and throws the plates as shards.
- A landed blow cuts a four-point star, a turning hexagon and a spray of shards, scaled by damage.
- Consumables use their painted inventory art in the fight: drawn from the pouch into the hand, then thrown, poured or planted. Cinder Charge burns a fuse ring down before a faceted blast; Veil Canister and Laureate Shroud leave a soft cloud in their own colours; poison is poured over the weapon hand and leaves wisps while the coating lasts; the Waypoint Anchor plants a thread that shortens with the time left.
- Chakra charge and ward use the same cut-crystal look. Items, the blast, the warp and a guard break have their own sounds.

## Controls and compatibility

- Controls are unchanged: guard, parry timing, item use and item cycling work as before.
- These are presentation changes. Combat rules, damage, timings, the online protocol and the rules version are unchanged, so saved loadouts and online play remain compatible with v0.40.1.

## Limits

- Each fighter sheet has one frame per attack phase, so attack poses still snap between phases.
- Stages are single paintings without parallax layers, and sprite and stage art were not regenerated in this release. The lower part of the three repaired exports is softer than a fresh export.
- Unreleased preview rivals still use the procedural body and its line-drawn weapons.
- Verified in automated headless browsers on the WebGL and Canvas renderers; physical phone and controller behaviour remains a separate check. Production deploys only to the existing Render game.
