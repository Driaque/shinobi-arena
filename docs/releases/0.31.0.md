# Fabled Rivals v0.31.0

- Open Story & Co-op from the arena toolbar to enter Adventures.
- Northern Echoes is a three-encounter solo chapter featuring Silent and Winta, with original-roster opponents, dialogue, a closing scene and checkpoints saved on this device. Choose a difficulty, continue your checkpoint or restart. Each encounter uses a fixed story kit and ordinary first-to-two duel rules.
- Hold the Terrace is private two-player online survival. Create a room, share its eight-character code, and have your friend join on another device. Both players fight two AI enemies per wave across three waves. Friendly fire is disabled; targeting, projectiles and ground effects distinguish teams.
- Clearing a wave restores fallen allies, recovers health and refreshes kits before the next wave. Both players share victory after wave three, or defeat when both fall or the wave timer expires.

## Controls and compatibility

Adventures supports keyboard and simultaneous touch buttons. A/D move, Z/L depth, W jump, Q/E dash, J basic, H weapon, K guard, U/I/O techniques, R Replacement, G charge and T awaken. Story can pause, including on tab blur; online co-op continues while a player is away. Each device controls its own ally using the same keys. The current Canvas/Pixi presentation is reused for all four fighters.

## Limits

The first story chapter is a short sequence of arena encounters, without exploration or voiced cinematics. Story checkpoints are local and do not follow Discord accounts. Co-op uses fixed default kits for four original selectable heroes. No local couch co-op, in-wave revive, reconnect or boss-specific mechanics yet. Co-op rooms are private by shared code and are omitted from public spectator listings. If either ally disconnects, the expedition ends and players must create a new room. Co-op does not affect PvP rating, match history, replays, tournament rewards or progression challenges. Story and co-op require the matching frontend/server release; existing duels retain their rules.
