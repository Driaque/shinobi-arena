# Fabled Rivals v0.31.0

- Choose a battle stage before CPU, local two-player and Practice fights. Skyglass Terrace is joined by Frostbound Pass, Ember Forge, Moonlit Grove and Sunken Court. Random resolves to one of these five stages when a match is created.
- Stage cards show previews and descriptions. The chosen preference is saved on this device; active matches lock the picker until a new match can begin.
- New stages use distinct vector scenery and themed snow, ember or ambient motes in the shared Canvas/Pixi battlefield. Original Canvas fallback also draws the selected environment.
- Online matchmaking uses the first queued player's stage preference. The server resolves it once, and both players and spectators receive that stage. Recorded state frames retain the stage for replay viewing.

## Controls and compatibility

Select a card under Select battle stage, then use the existing fight/queue button. All stages share combat dimensions, ground, lanes, damage and movement rules. There are no hazards or gameplay advantages. Stage identity persists across rounds and Practice resets. Practice's Aren appearance preview keeps its fixed demonstration arena.

## Limits

The four new environments are vector artwork; Skyglass retains its existing painted background. New clients are needed to see the new environments; older recordings without a stage use Skyglass. Online stage voting and vetoes, environmental hazards, unique audio and stage-specific story encounters are not included.
