# Shinobi Arena v0.19.0

## Movement, space and readability

- The arena is 1600 world units wide (25% wider), with depth increased to ±64. Uniform fighter scale is 0.86, with matching body dimensions and unchanged movement speed. Vector details remain, and archived replays retain their recorded arena and scale.
- Ground markers, lane guides, attack preparation labels and recovery cues explain spacing and counter opportunities. Feel settings offer reduced particles and screen shake.
- Standard browser gamepads support movement/depth and combat, with sixteen remappable buttons under Feel. A second connected controller operates local P2. Keyboard and touch remain available.
- Analog dead zone, sensitivity, double-pull timing, mirrored left-handed layout, floating stick and compact controls are saved per device. The equipped row has size/offset settings and a one-touch Crouch Guard. Optional hold-drag-release aiming directs projectiles and field placement; pointer cancellation does not cast.

## Tactical loadouts and combos

- Authored jutsu routes connect native signature attacks and shared techniques. Clean melee confirms can cancel into an authored equipped follow-up, with normal costs, cooldowns, aerial limits, at most two authored cancels and ordinary guard/escape answers. Setup/projectile routes are tactical sequences, not guaranteed true combos.
- The strategy advisor recommends compatible techniques, labels move roles, shows equipped routes and chakra warnings, supplies Balanced/Aerial/Control starter builds, and saves up to 30 named builds on this browser. Limits remain seven jutsus, two weapons and four consumables.
- Live meta uses only the current rules version’s latest 500 completed rated matches within 30 days. Recommendations require a pair to appear in 20 matches across five opponent pairs. Counts and co-equipped loadout win rates describe association, not causal strength; authored advice remains available without data. Casual matches, early forfeits and old rules are excluded.

## Learning and online play

- Post-match coaching summarizes damage, parries, recovery punishes and jutsu links, suggests a specific improvement and opens Practice. Existing practice instructions now match the depth joystick.
- Practice adds projectile, aerial and counter dummies, an up-to-10-second action recorder with looping opponent playback, and adjustable offline practice speed.
- Replays support bookmarks, previous/next recorded samples and P1 practice takeover at the preceding recorded snapshot for this rules version. Older snapshots remain watchable; takeover does not write an online result or modify the archive.
- Online play displays measured RTT and jitter. Optional experimental movement prediction replays up to 12 unacknowledged local input ticks from an authoritative snapshot and corrects on acknowledgements. Only local movement is predicted for presentation; HP, resources, hits and ranking remain server-authoritative. This is not full rollback netcode. Very long backlogs fall back to authoritative presentation.

## Compatibility and limits

- All devices must refresh: protocol 5 / duel-0.19.0. Release History includes this version automatically. Profiles, ranks and saved controls/loadouts remain valid.
- Artwork remains procedural 2D. The expanded arena does not add ring-outs or a free-roaming 3D camera. Meta recommendations need real rated results before becoming available. Audio still requires a user gesture. The free PostgreSQL database expires November 27, 2026.
