# Fabled Rivals v0.42.2

## Battle stages

- Skyglass Terrace, Ember Forge, Moonlit Grove and Sunken Court have new painted floors that extend beneath the front and rear movement lanes.
- The updated paintings appear in stage selection and in battle with Canvas and WebGL. Frostbound Pass retains the extended ice floor from v0.42.1.

## Controls, compatibility and limits

- Select any arena through the normal Battle Stage picker. Touch, keyboard and controller movement work as before.
- This release updates the scenery. Arena boundaries, lane depth, combat rules, saved loadouts and the online protocol retain v0.42.1 compatibility.
- Tall or short screens crop the scenery to fit the viewport. Floor coverage was checked in portrait and landscape browser emulation.
