# Fabled Rivals v0.28.0

## Detailed original fighter artwork

- All ten originals now have painted combat sprite sheets: Silent, Winta, Dora, Aren, Eira, Kael, Nyra, Rook, Veyra and Oryn. Metal, layered clothing, hair and character accessories replace the simpler bodies once their artwork loads.
- Each fighter has 16 authored poses covering idle, running, dash, jump/landing, attack anticipation/strike/recovery, aerial attack, guard, hit reaction, awakening and casting. Existing short pose blends and presentation motion remain active. These are pose sheets, not unique animation sequences for every jutsu.
- Silent retains his mask, silver hair and northern armor. Winta has crimson armor; Dora has royal teal attire. Aren keeps his dark skin, white locs and teal/gold silhouette in his refreshed artwork. Silent and Winta remain blood brothers in the story.
- The main renderer uses the selected fighter's sheet in CPU, local, Training and online fights, with the same presentation in live spectating and replay viewing. Mirror matches keep separate player slots. The standalone Visual Lab and explicit Aren/sparring-echo Practice appearance remain the earlier comparison prototype.

## Loading and compatibility

- Only sheets for fighters encountered in the current view load. Matching identities share their image/texture; changing fighters does not restart the fight. Images are normalized to a common foot anchor and calibrated to the existing body scale.
- Missing or invalid sheets keep the existing character appearance. Graphics reports the failed identity; **Graphics → Retry graphics** retries without resetting combat. Unsupported/lost WebGL uses Canvas with the same loaded artwork. Closing a viewer releases its textures and ignores late image loads.
- Player 2 changes now refresh the sampled model even when the simulation tick has not advanced.
- Controls, loadouts, hitboxes, damage, ranked rules, protocol and replay results are unchanged. Weapons, status effects and combat text still use the existing compatibility layer; these images do not provide weapon-swappable skeletal rigs. Animated selection thumbnails are preserved.

## Limits and validation

- Full-body sprites prioritize gameplay readability at the existing fighter size. The 16 poses share a common action structure; bespoke choreography, full facial rigs and new per-jutsu animation clips remain future work.
- The automated verification includes atlas loading/failure/disposal, normalized scale, mirrors and selected identities. Browser gates exercise desktop/portrait, native WebGL/Canvas, missing-sheet retry and graphics recovery, alongside the existing gameplay suites. Physical iPhone performance still needs measurement.
