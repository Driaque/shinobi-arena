# Fabled Rivals v0.40.0

## An original world

- The move library, awakening forms, weapons and consumables now use names from Fabled Rivals. Lore and artwork emphasize the Skyglass world, its ancient cultures and conduit technology.
- Twenty-five elite future rivals have distinct identities, histories and visual designs. Enable Include unreleased rivals to preview them; the ten core rivals remain the default. Preview rivals use standard competitive stats and may change before their full release.

## Painted techniques and inventory

- Techniques, weapons and consumables receive responsive painted PNG illustrations, including separate 64, 128 and 256 pixel exports. Future-rival portraits have 192, 384 and 768 pixel exports.
- Action names, costs, cooldowns, selected states and accessible button text remain live interface elements. Failed images retain usable fallback controls.

## Compatibility and limits

- Existing saved loadout selections are migrated to the renamed content IDs when loaded. Online protocol 8 and the new rules version require older tabs to refresh.
- Future combat bodies are original articulated designs; portrait artwork is not a new combat sprite animation set.
- Existing cooldowns, equipment limits and combat balance apply. Production publication is limited to the existing Render service.
