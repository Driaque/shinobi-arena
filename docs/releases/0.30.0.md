# Fabled Rivals v0.30.0

- Enter the player lounge from Online mode to see connected players and guests, their fighter, availability and rating record.
- Challenge an available rival. The recipient accepts or declines; the sender can cancel. Invitations expire after 30 seconds and close when either player leaves or starts a fight.
- Accepted duels use the selected loadouts and casual rules. They do not change ranked rating.
- Send a respectful bow, hello or ready gesture during the countdown, and good fight, well played or another duel gestures after a round or match. Messages reach your opponent, with a two-second cooldown.
- Profiles now show a player card, rank, rating and fight record. Save your display name without losing your profile or progress.

## Controls and compatibility

Choose Online Matchmaking, select your equipment, enter your player name and press Enter player lounge. View profile expands a player's public record. Challenge, Accept duel, Decline and Cancel challenge work with touch or mouse. Find opponent still enters ordinary matchmaking. Cancel search leaves the lounge. Profile / sign in opens your personal profile.

## Limits

Presence lists connections to this server, not everyone visiting the website. Guests can join without Discord. Lounge loadouts are captured on entry; use Refresh lounge loadout after equipment changes. Invitations are one at a time and sending is limited to once every five seconds. Gestures are preset text messages, not fighter animations, and are disabled during combat. Another duel is a gesture, not an automatic rematch. Profile summaries in the lounge reflect the record at connection time. Guest identities and recovery codes remain private. Server restarts clear presence and invitations. No friend list or free-text chat is included.
