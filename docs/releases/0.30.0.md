# Fabled Rivals v0.30.0

- Aren and Silent gain distinct continuous sword wind-up, swing and recovery keys. Their forearms use a two-bone solve to keep elbows and wrists connected, and blades rotate with the moving hand.
- Their idle, running, airborne and guard silhouettes differ. Hair, scarf and ties use bounded secondary motion.
- Hits and guards emit distinct expanding sparks. Landing rings follow fighter depth; running dust, dash streaks and charge rings add movement cues. Impact camera shake decays and scales with damage.
- Animation Review is accessible beside Visual Lab. Compare both fighters, choose a movement or combo, slow playback, pause, step one tick, show joints or mirror poses.

## Controls and compatibility

Use the existing combat controls. Select Continuous fighter animation in Graphics for the new poses. Reduced-motion mode suppresses decorative battle motion, and the existing effects/shake settings remain independent. Review playback starts paused with reduced motion.

Combat timings, damage, hitboxes, networking and replay schema are unchanged. The review reads actual move durations but is a presentation preview, not a simulated fight. It cannot establish attack reach or collision correctness.

## Limits

This is an articulated Canvas animation pass for Aren and Silent. Painted sprite sheets and other fighters have not been reauthored. No simulation hit-stop, Spine dependency, new soundtrack or device FPS guarantee is included. Wider art refinement and real-device playtesting remain ongoing work.
