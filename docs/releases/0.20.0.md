# Shinobi Arena v0.20.0

## Clearer mobile combat

- Practice hints can be hidden with Hide on the panel or Hide hints in the toolbar, and restored with Show hints. The preference survives reloads and applies to free/recorded practice and drills. Hiding hints does not stop or reset the dummy.
- All 64 selectable jutsus have original vector pictograms, combining technique silhouettes, elemental colours and variant details. Loadout cards, the equipped row, the expanded jutsu menu and signature controls share the artwork. Mobile uses compact icon buttons with cooldown/key indicators; full names remain in accessible labels and tooltips. Icons load locally, without remote images.
- The equipped row keeps 48 px touch buttons and stays in the control deck. Existing visibility/opacity settings remain available. Tournament reward gear can be previewed offline and is checked against profile ownership online.

## Tournaments

- Arena → Tournaments offers public signup, player-created 2-, 4- and 8-player single-elimination brackets, randomized seeding and normal best-of-three battles against the exact bracket opponent. The organizer starts a full bracket. Players can leave signup; organizers can cancel registration. Pending players can withdraw and advance their opponent; leaving a running fight forfeits it.
- Brackets progress only from server-run results. Tournament queues are separate from casual/ranked queues. Existing live spectators and archived replays work for tournament battles. Tournament results do not alter Elo.
- Brackets and rewards are stored persistently. Interrupted pairings reopen after a server restart; interrupted battles restart rather than resume. A player may join one active tournament, create up to five events per UTC day, and registration expires after seven days. Active event capacity is bounded.

## Points and exclusive equipment

- An eligible champion receives 100 tournament points and Champion’s Katana, a gold variant using standard Katana combat stats. An eligible runner-up receives 40 points and Victory Smoke, a variant using standard Smoke Bomb behavior. Other eligible entrants receive 10 points.
- Eligibility requires at least 20 seconds of server-run tournament play. At most three completed tournament reward opportunities per profile per UTC day are recorded. Awards and match results are committed atomically and cannot be claimed twice. Withdrawals and zero-play forfeits cannot manufacture eligible rewards.
- Rewards appear in Arena → My profile and Tournaments; ownership follows the saved profile recovery code. Points have no spending shop in this version. Gear unlocks are permanent in this database, not consumable reward claims. Mobile/browser storage alone cannot grant online ownership.

## Compatibility and limits

- Refresh all devices: protocol 6 / duel-0.20.0. Existing profiles, ranks, saved controls/loadouts and historical replays remain valid. Tournaments require healthy persistent storage. The free PostgreSQL database expires November 27, 2026.
- This release does not add scheduled tournaments, automatic no-show adjudication, cash prizes, a points shop or mid-fight recovery after server restarts. Players arrange a time, sign up and check in through the bracket. Original pictograms are symbolic technique art rather than anime screenshots.
