# Shinobi Arena v0.16.0

## Aerial combat and fluid animation

- 58-jutsu library: Rising Gale launches from ground or air; Sky Cleave, Diving Flame and Aerial Water Senbon are aerial-only. Cards label AERIAL ONLY, GROUND / AIR, GROUNDED, AIR COMBO, LAUNCHER and AIR FINISHER; tags are searchable.
- Confirm a launcher, then tap Jump during its recovery to spend a normal jump and chase. A whiff or guarded hit cannot jump-cancel. Basic becomes a three-stage air string; confirmed basics can chain into equipped aerial jutsus. Sky Cleave can confirm into another equipped aerial jutsu. Chakra, stamina and cooldowns still apply.
- Four air actions per landing; a double jump does not reset the allowance. After four juggle hits, the defender falls with air recovery and cannot be hit again in the air until landing. Replacement remains an escape. Dive finishers drive the target down. Landing cancels aerial-only attacks with six ticks of recovery.
- Air steering eases into its target speed. Visual interpolation smooths local and online fighter positions without moving hitboxes. Blended anticipation, follow-through, airborne leg poses, leaning, secondary cloth motion, dash afterimages and landing dust improve movement readability.
- Detailed procedural fighters add vest layers, seams, collar trim, belt ties, utility pouches, sandal straps and face details. Moonlit Shrine adds cedar layers, shrine posts, rope, stone lanterns, perspective flagstones, moss, grasses and atmospheric haze.
- Reduced-motion preference suppresses idle bob, running oscillation, trailing afterimages and camera shake. Combat poses and hitboxes remain readable.

## Compatibility and limits

- Refresh both devices. Protocol 3 uses rules duel-0.16.0; older clients cannot join new matches. Archived replays continue to use their recorded state and original catalogs.
- Loadout limits stay seven jutsus, two weapons and four consumables. Existing saved loadouts and control settings remain valid. Mobile uses the same analog up gesture, Basic and Jutsus palette; no additional permanent buttons.
- Art remains procedural 2D animation; bespoke frame-by-frame character sprites are future work. Online play remains authoritative snapshot networking, without rollback prediction.
- The free PostgreSQL database still expires November 27, 2026. Export/migrate before expiry to retain profiles and history.
