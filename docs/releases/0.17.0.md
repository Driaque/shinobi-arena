# Shinobi Arena v0.17.0

## Crouch guarding and battlefield scale

- Hold Crouch and Guard together: S + K on P1 defaults, Down + M for P2, or hold the mobile analog down and Guard with a second finger. The HUD explicitly shows CROUCH GUARD.
- Blocked-hit recovery now keeps a held guard and crouch stance active. Real hitstun and guard breaks still prevent defending. Releasing Guard exposes the fighter; crouching alone does not auto-block. Front-facing guard, chip damage, guard meter, parries and guard-piercing attacks retain their existing rules.
- Live fighters are 12% smaller. Body width, standing/crouched projectile hurtboxes, projectile spawn heights and melee vertical tolerance scale with their models. Attack reach, movement speed, HP and damage remain unchanged.
- Smaller fighters leave more room to read Moonlit Shrine scenery and aerial movement. Old replay fighters retain their recorded scale; recordings from before scaling render at their original size.

## Included aerial and animation improvements

- Includes v0.16’s 58-jutsu library, searchable aerial/combo tags, launcher jump chases, aerial basic strings, Sky Cleave, Diving Flame, Rising Gale and Aerial Water Senbon. Four air actions per landing and four-hit juggle recovery prevent infinite air strings.
- Fighter and projectile interpolation, continuous attack poses, cloth/hair motion, afterimages, landing dust, character-specific clothing/equipment and Moonlit Shrine depth remain included. Art remains procedural 2D.

## Compatibility

- Refresh both devices. Protocol 3 now uses duel-0.17.0. Older snapshots/catalogs remain playable as replays. Existing loadouts, controls, ratings and profiles remain valid.
- The free PostgreSQL database still expires November 27, 2026; export/migrate before expiry to retain online data.
