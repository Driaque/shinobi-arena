# Fabled Rivals v0.42.1

## Mobile combat

- Expanded touch shows Weapon, the current Item, the first two equipped Jutsus, and Replacement in a fixed bottom row. Tap them directly in portrait or landscape.
- The lower row shows secondary actions. More cycles that row in place. It leaves the primary row available.
- Players with a saved dock or custom overlay can switch under Settings → Controls → Placement → Expanded touch and save the selection.

## Frost battlefield

- Frost uses new painted scenery with a continuous ice surface below both movement lanes, visible in the preview and on the Canvas and WebGL battlefield.

## Responsiveness and compatibility

- The closed jutsu tray skips UI refreshes, and the performance status updates four times per second.
- Add `?touchTiming=1` to enable the `touch-to-render` User Timing measure. It ends at local frame submission, or at frame submission after the server acknowledges an online input. The measure excludes display scanout.
- Saved controls and loadouts, simulation rules, and the online protocol are compatible with v0.42.0.

## Limits and next checks

- Physical-device timing and online latency on players’ connections still need measurement. This update makes no claim of faster network response.
- Frost is the first floor-art update. Other arenas use their existing paintings.
