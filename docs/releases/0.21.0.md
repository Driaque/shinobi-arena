# Shinobi Arena v0.21.0

## Eight-character benchmark pilot

- Arena → Tournaments displays benchmark events with league standings and a seeded knockout bracket. Eight entrants play seven league rounds, each facing every other entrant once: 28 league matches and seven knockout matches, each using normal best-of-three battle rules.
- League sides are balanced: each entrant starts on each side three or four times. Wins earn three league points. Ties use mini-league wins among entrants tied on points, round difference, damage difference, then name. Quarterfinals pair seeds 1–8, 4–5, 2–7 and 3–6; semifinal paths preserve these seeds.
- Rankings describe performance within this AI pilot and its recorded loadouts. They do not assign permanent roster power tiers or prove human competitive balance. The pilot controller uses the same Xtreme policy and equipment budget across entrants; native jutsus vary by character. Future groups have no results until played.

## Online records and fairness

- Benchmark match results progress through the existing authoritative online tournament queue. Only completed battles count toward benchmark standings; interruptions reopen the pairing. Each entrant keeps the same character throughout and cannot duplicate another entrant’s character.
- Benchmark results do not change player Elo, player game/win/loss statistics, tournament points, gear ownership or daily reward opportunities. Normal player tournaments retain their existing behavior and rewards.
- Completed pairings offer Replay fight directly from the tournament card. League tables, seeds, scorelines and champion persist in the existing database. Replays retain the current latest-200 / 14-day retention limit; older replay buttons may report expiry.

## Controls, compatibility and limits

- Combat controls and rules are unchanged: protocol 6 / duel-0.20.0. Refresh the page to load the updated tournament viewer. Existing profiles, normal tournaments and archived fights remain compatible.
- The initial benchmark is launched by the project test runner; the public signup UI continues creating normal 2-, 4- and 8-player knockout tournaments. Benchmark events are labelled and have no public signup/fight buttons. No paid infrastructure, cash rewards or permanent global character rating is introduced. The current preview database expires November 27, 2026.
