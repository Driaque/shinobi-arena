# Fabled Rivals v0.30.1

- Arena → Challenges now shows three daily objectives, a weekly objective and permanent First steps progress.
- Completed online matches of at least 20 seconds count when both players have saved profiles and both dealt damage in the final round. The first three matches against an opponent per UTC day count; forfeits, self-matches and automated character benchmarks do not.
- Earn cosmetic New Challenger, Daily Rival and Weekly Contender titles. Complete all three daily objectives for Daily Rival. Equip an unlocked title in Challenges and see it in My profile. Titles do not alter combat or equipment.
- Claim one login day per UTC day. Seven cumulative days unlock Returning Rival. Missing days never resets the track; the completed track does not repeat.

## Controls and compatibility

Open Arena, choose Challenges, then claim your login or equip an earned title. Refresh progress after a fight. Guest profiles are supported; existing Discord linking carries the same saved player progress across devices. Daily objectives reset at midnight UTC; weeks start Monday UTC. Existing gameplay controls are unchanged.

## Limits and planned work

Reward claims require healthy persistent server storage. Arcade, local duels and training do not award progress in this release. Titles currently display in My profile and Challenges. This is basic anti-farming protection, not comprehensive collusion detection. Story chapters, co-op, colour variants and gestures remain future work. Existing combat and online reliability priorities remain ahead of larger modes.
