# Fabled Rivals v0.34.0

Match preparation now has three steps: Fighter, Loadout, and Battlefield & Ready. Next confirms each step; Back and the step tabs preserve selections. Existing saved builds remain compatible. Changing fighters still validates their available techniques. Customize jutsus, weapons and consumables through the loadout library, or use the current build.

Local versus requires player two to confirm the proposed stage or Random. Changing the stage clears that confirmation. CPU and training let you choose. Casual online players submit independent builds before pairing, then both accept the same stage preference or Random between their preferences. A 60-second agreement timeout cancels preparation without awarding a result. Inputs cannot advance combat during preparation. Builds cannot be changed after pairing; leave and prepare again.

Ranked and tournament matches use server-selected random stages from the current five-stage pool. Stages currently share identical combat geometry and differ visually. Story encounters keep their authored stages; co-op survival uses its system-selected stage. These separate adventure flows retain their mission preparation screens.

Preparation controls support keyboard, desktop and phone portrait and landscape. Online players must refresh to the matching client/server version before connecting. No matchmaking rewards are added by this update.
