# Fabled Rivals v0.35.0

## Detailed running fighters and steady framing

- All ten originals use the approved full-body eight-frame running cycles. Empty-handed and equipped carry variants share fixed roots and proportions, with no cut-apart limbs or translucent pose crossfades. Running cadence follows distance travelled in both Canvas and PixiJS.
- Painted fighters are now the default. Settings → Graphics → Fighter animation still offers Continuous fighters. This version introduces a new preference key so existing players see the updated default once.
- Equipped carry art appears when the selected weapon matches that character’s painted weapon and both poison and smoke items remain available. Silent’s dual carry requires Tachi and Kaiken. Other loadouts use the empty-handed sprint. Nyra’s apothecary accessories and Oryn’s lantern remain part of their character design.
- The arena keeps a fixed scale throughout jumps, double jumps and landings. Aerial headroom is reserved when fitting the viewport, removing jump-triggered shrinking.
- Continuous fighters retain distance-driven gait, connected arms, landing weight shift and fractional attack posing. Painted action root motion flows through anticipation, strike and recovery.

## Controls and compatibility

- Controls, collision boxes, damage, movement speeds and combat timings are unchanged. Selected fighter presentation applies across fights, training and Adventures, with Canvas fallback and reduced-motion support.
- Only selected characters’ sheets load. An unavailable running sheet retains the existing painted poses; Retry graphics reloads failed art.
- Story and co-op survival share normal fight controls. Teammates start in separate lanes, with formation restored between waves.

## Limits

- This art pass supplies running cycles. Idle, attacks, guards, jumps and reactions retain existing artwork; their silhouettes can differ when transitioning into or out of a run.
- Equipped frames depict specific carry sets, not arbitrary weapons or layered inventory rendering. Different loadouts use the empty-handed running variant.
- Short landscape viewports start with wider fixed framing to reserve aerial headroom. Extreme launches may still extend beyond the reserved area.
