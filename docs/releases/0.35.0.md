# Fabled Rivals v0.35.0

## Fighter movement and camera

- The arena keeps a fixed scale throughout jumps, double jumps and landings. Aerial headroom is reserved when the viewport is fitted, so fighters no longer shrink whenever someone jumps.
- Continuous fighters use a distance-driven gait with planted stance feet, eased return steps, knee articulation, restrained body rise and a landing weight shift. Arms remain connected across the roster; sword poses retain anticipation and follow-through.
- Local attack poses advance between simulation ticks alongside movement for smoother hands and weapons on high refresh displays.
- Painted sprites retain opaque silhouettes and consistent proportions. Their root motion now meets continuously through anticipation, strike and recovery, with a shared running rhythm for Canvas and PixiJS.

## Controls and compatibility

- Controls, collision boxes, damage, movement speeds and combat timings are unchanged. Presentation changes apply to normal fights, training and Adventures, with the existing Canvas fallback and reduced-motion preference preserved.
- Story and co-op survival use normal fight controls. Teammates start in separate lanes, with formation restored between waves.

## Limits

- The painted sheets still contain 16 key poses, including two running silhouettes. This update adds no new painted frames or layered sprite artwork. A future art pass needs authored in-betweens or layered rigs to match the continuous fighters' articulation.
- Short landscape viewports use a wider fixed framing from the start to preserve aerial headroom. Extreme launch combinations may still extend beyond that reserved area.
