# Fabled Rivals v0.37.0

## Controller play and a clearer battlefield

- Controller activity hides the touch deck and expands the rendered battlefield into its space in versus, training, story and online co-op survival. Health, energy, equipped ability cooldowns and controller shortcuts remain visible. Arena boundaries and combat rules stay the same.
- Touch the screen to restore touch controls. A connected but idle controller does not take over the layout. Settings → Feel → Show touch controls with controller keeps both available; this preference saves on the device.
- Connection status and PlayStation/Xbox button prompts help identify the active input. Two controllers retain separate player assignments in local versus. Solo and online modes accept only the local player's controller.
- Disconnecting or leaving the browser clears controller holds and pending presses. Returning from the background requires neutral controls first. Menu confirmation held into a fight does not become a jump or attack.

## Default controller controls

- Left stick moves horizontally and between depth lanes; right stick aims compatible projectiles.
- Cross/A jumps, Circle/B uses the weapon, Square/X attacks, Triangle/Y dashes forward.
- L1/LB guards, L2/LT replaces, R2/RT charges chakra.
- Hold R1/RB, then Cross/A, Circle/B, Square/X or Triangle/Y for equipped ability slots 1–4. Hold R1/RB plus D-pad up, down or left for slots 5–7.
- D-pad up moves to the rear lane, down crouches, left dashes backward, right uses the selected consumable.
- Create/View cycles consumables, L3/LS swaps weapons, R3/RS awakens. Options/Menu pauses offline play; online matches continue.
- In menus, D-pad or left stick moves focus, Cross/A confirms and Circle/B closes a dialog or menu. A focused select cycles options with directions; confirm moves to the next control. Text fields still use the device keyboard.
- Settings → Feel includes saved button remapping, analog sensitivity/dead zone and Restore default controller mapping. Existing customized button mappings are preserved; restore defaults to adopt the new ability modifier.

## Compatibility and limits

Pair a controller in the operating system's Bluetooth settings or connect it by USB, then press a button while the game has focus. Use a browser exposing the controller through the Gamepad API. PS5 DualSense, Xbox and compatible generic pads use the browser's reported layout; nonstandard pads may need remapping. Compatibility varies by browser, operating system, firmware and connection. This release does not add adaptive triggers, touchpad gestures or rumble. Automated browser tests simulate controller input; physical iOS, Android, DualSense and Xbox testing remains a separate hardware check.
