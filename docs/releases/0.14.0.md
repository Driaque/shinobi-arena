# Shinobi Arena v0.14.0

## Dora — AquaPrincess joins the arena

- Dora is playable for P1, local P2, all CPU difficulties, training and online matchmaking. Find her under Originals / TNR or search Dora / AquaPrincess. The roster now has 19 fighters.
- Her concept follows the supplied brief: Silent’s wife, a graceful, smart, strong heroine controlling wind, water and fire. Light golden-brown skin, brown eyes, long dark hair, regal aqua/navy/gold clothing, necklace and earrings. Profile height: 5′7″.
- Selecting Dora equips Bladed Fans and Katana. Both weapon slots and all four consumable slots remain editable. Bladed Fans are also available to other fighters.
- Her three-hit fan chain uses the normal timing, stamina and damage scaling. Holding Basic does not automatically chain attacks.
- Tidal Edict: water projectile, 65 damage, 25 chakra, 3.5-second cooldown; clean hits weaken enemy attacks by 25% for three seconds. Guard or Replacement prevents the debuff.
- Royal Tempest: 40 chakra, six-second cooldown. A clean wind opener deals 35 damage, weakens by 20% for 2.5 seconds and pulls into a 130-damage fire finisher. Its 18-tick startup follows 12-tick hitstun: the opponent can guard, parry, interrupt or use Replacement. Blocking, replacing or dodging the opener prevents the follow-up. It is not a guaranteed combo.
- Triune Flow: 30 chakra, three-second stance, eight-second cooldown. Hold Guard after activation; a guarded hit readies one +35% fan or sword counter for 1.5 seconds. It grants no passive immunity, reflect or healing. Full pierce bypasses it.
- Limit Release: ready after 400 cumulative actual HP lost, manually activate once per round while free to act. Ten seconds of +10% damage/movement, 15% faster basic timing, burning Royal Gale and stronger Sovereign Vortex / Blazing Crown. No healing or invulnerability.
- Her victory message includes “Yaaay!” The stage fighter has a distinct robe, jewelry, fans, long hair and awakening aura. Detailed animated sprites are still future work; the portrait is concept art.

## Controls and compatibility

- P1 defaults: J Basic, H weapon, U Tidal Edict, I Royal Tempest, O Triune Flow, T Limit Release, F swap weapons. Mobile uses the corresponding labeled buttons; saved mappings and visibility settings still apply.
- Both online players must refresh after deployment. Protocol remains 2; combat rules advance to duel-0.14.0. Older clients are rejected rather than joining incompatible fights.
- Weaken preserves its actual percentage when spawning projectiles, matching melee damage rules.
- Move names and balance numbers are proposed gameplay adaptations of the supplied character brief.

## Still planned
