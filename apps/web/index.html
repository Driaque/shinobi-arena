How to play & prototype notes

Land hits to reduce HP. Guard while grounded to reduce incoming damage; it still causes a little chip damage. Specials use chakra, which slowly regenerates. Tap attacks and specials again for the next move—holding them does not repeat. Jump over fireballs, or guard them. A double knockout or equal HP at time expiry is a draw. Hitstun interrupts attacks.

Phone: hold sideways; use your left thumb to move and your right thumb to fight. Rotate, switch apps, or lose focus to pause. Tap Resume to continue. Local two-player requires a shared keyboard. No online play yet.

Art is procedural placeholder animation, not anime sprite artwork. Computer strategy is a simple deterministic baseline. Character selection, remappable keys, audio and online matches are future work.
