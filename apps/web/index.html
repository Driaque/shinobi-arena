Mechanics, controls & prototype notes

Double jump: release and press jump again in the air. Double-tap left/right within 14 ticks to dash in that direction, or use Q/E (back/forward) and the touch dash buttons. Dash is grounded and grants no invulnerability.

Replacement (R): spend 25 chakra to escape hitstun, retreat and avoid direct hits briefly. Eight-second cooldown. It does not cure poison or burn. Pierce bypasses guard; it does not bypass Replacement. Poison and burn deal damage each second; weakening reduces outgoing damage by 30%. Reapplying the same effect refreshes its duration without stacking its strength. Status timers appear under HP.

Basic (J), equipped weapon (H), signatures (U/I) and bloodline (O) are separate attacks. Guard while grounded to reduce damage. Tap actions again after recovery; holding does not repeat. Specials spend chakra and have cooldowns. Blue technique timers mean cooldown; greyed actions mean insufficient chakra or no bloodline.

P2: left/right arrows move and double-tap dash, up arrow double jumps, N basic, B weapon, M guard, comma/period signatures, slash bloodline, V Replacement. On mobile, hold sideways and use touch controls. One phone controls P1; choose the computer opponent. Changing modes starts a fresh loadout. Rematch lets you change characters and weapons.
