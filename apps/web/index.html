Mechanics, controls & prototype notes

Use the analog stick for movement: a small push walks slowly, a full push moves at full speed. Pull up to jump, return to center, then pull up again to double jump. Hold down to crouch; return to center to stand. Double-pull left or right within 300 ms to dash in that screen direction. Holding a direction never repeats jumps/dashes. Double jump: release and press jump again in the air. Double-tap left/right within 14 ticks to dash in that direction, or use Q/E (back/forward) and the touch dash buttons. Dash works on the ground or once in the air per jump sequence; it grants no invulnerability and costs 18 stamina. Jump cancels a dash, and fast movement presses are buffered briefly.

Replacement (R): spend 25 chakra to escape hitstun, retreat and avoid direct hits briefly. Eight-second cooldown. It does not cure poison or burn. Pierce bypasses guard; it does not bypass Replacement. Poison and burn deal damage each second; weakening reduces outgoing damage by 30%. Reapplying the same effect refreshes its duration without stacking its strength. Status timers appear under HP.

Basic (J), equipped weapon (H), jutsu slots 1–3 (U/I/O) are separate attacks. Guard while grounded to reduce damage. Tap actions again after recovery; holding does not repeat. Specials spend chakra and have cooldowns. Blue technique timers mean cooldown; greyed actions mean insufficient chakra or no bloodline.

P2: left/right arrows move and double-tap dash, up arrow double jumps, N basic, B weapon, M guard, comma/period signatures, slash bloodline, V Replacement. On mobile, landscape is recommended; either orientation supports the analog stick and action buttons. Open Controls to adjust opacity, choose a separate control deck below the fight or an overlay, drag and resize controls, or rebind keyboard keys; Save keeps the layout on this device. Separate layouts are kept for small, medium and large screens. P and Escape remain pause keys. One phone controls your fighter; choose Online Matchmaking to fight another device, or the computer opponent to play solo. Changing modes starts a fresh loadout. Rematch lets you change characters and weapons.

INVENTORY: Pick two weapons and four one-use consumables before starting or joining the queue. F swaps weapons; C chooses a consumable slot; X uses it. P2 uses 7/8/9. Swap and item actions have startup/recovery and cannot cancel committed attacks or escape hitstun. Items refill next round. Paper bombs explode after a delay, smoke blocks ordinary enemy projectiles, poison coats your next weapon attack, and marked Flying Raijin kunai can be used again to warp for 15 chakra. Controls includes a checkbox list to hide individual touch controls.

TRAINING: Choose Training in Mode. Practice selects idle, guarding or attacking dummies, resource refills, instant cooldowns and range overlays. Reset/Retry instantly resets the arena. Four guided challenges teach double jump → air dash, three-hit basics, guard → weapon counter and awakening. Completion comes from actual combat actions. The coach explains misses and rejected inputs. Drills pause when you open Practice or Controls, leave the page.

CPU DIFFICULTY: Easy is the default, with a three-second opening, single attacks and long pauses. Medium adds two-hit combos. Difficult adds full combos and recovery punishes. Xtreme reacts fastest and applies tight pressure. All four use the same HP, damage, stamina, chakra and cooldown rules as you. Change Difficulty at any time; an active match pauses, then resumes with the new setting. The choice is saved on this device; local P2 ignores it.

TACTICS: Basics cost 12 stamina (16 for the third hit); weapons cost 16. Stop attacking for 0.75 seconds to begin recovering stamina. Block early for safety, or time Guard within five ticks of impact to parry non-piercing attacks. A parry opens a counterattack window and has a one-second cooldown. Sustained guard pressure breaks the guard meter; weapons and jutsus deplete it faster. Full pierce bypasses guard and parry. A clean hit during enemy attack recovery gains 20% damage. Blocked or missed melee attacks have extra recovery, so spacing and baiting matter.

ARENA MOVEMENT: Analog moves left/right and toward the back/front of the battlefield. Jump uses its dedicated button or W; Crouch uses its dedicated button or S. Z/L move in depth on the keyboard. Hold Charge in the equipped-technique row or G to restore chakra. Audio settings control music, effects and mute.

CROUCH GUARD: Hold Down (S / down on the mobile analog) and Guard together. Keep both held to retain the low shield through blocked-hit recovery. Guard meter, guard breaks and pierce still apply; crouching by itself does not block.

AERIAL COMBAT: Equip Rising Gale to launch, then tap Jump after a clean hit to chase. Basic becomes a three-stage aerial string while airborne. Confirm into Sky Cleave, or finish with Diving Flame. Aerial Water Senbon controls air space. Search AERIAL in the loadout library. Maximum four air attacks per landing; four juggle hits grant the defender air recovery. Replacement escapes hitstun. Aerial-only moves cannot start on the ground; ground fields require landing.

COMBOS: tap Basic three times with rhythm; a ten-tick input buffer catches early taps. After a confirmed basic hit, weapon and signature inputs can cancel recovery. Guards, whiffs, Replacement and recovery gaps break a combo. Later hits scale down in damage.

AWAKENING: selected fighters fill a meter from actual HP lost. When ready, press T (P1), Y (P2), or the Awaken button while free to act. One activation per round. No healing or damage immunity is granted. The form expires after its timer; Kurama changes Naruto’s signatures while active.

ORIGINALS / TNR: Dora — AquaPrincess uses fans, U Tidal Edict (water weaken), I Royal Tempest (pull then defendable heavy finisher), O Triune Flow (guard to ready a fan/sword counter), T Limit Release (wind/fire awakening). Selecting Dora suggests fans and katana; equipment stays editable. Silent and Winta have sword basic chains. Silent: U Harmonious Slash, I Echoing Rift (pull and metal vulnerability), O Tetsugan stance. Guard during the stance to absorb 20% of chip, reflect 30% of guardable attack power and ready a sword counter (+35%, 1.5 seconds). Full pierce bypasses this guard. Winta: U Mizukumi dash strike, I Eclipse Drain (35% lifesteal on clean hits), O Shinrai Ōu (+15% lightning damage for five seconds). Proposed awakenings change signatures for ten seconds. These are real-time gameplay adaptations; the duo ultimate is a future concept.

Procedural prototype artwork. Combat effects and stats are gameplay adaptations. Online play uses a separate authoritative multiplayer server. Display names are temporary labels, not accounts. Detailed anime sprite assets are not included yet. The round buttons and thumbstick take inspiration from mobile action-game controls, while combat remains a side-view fighter.
