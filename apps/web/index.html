Mechanics, controls & prototype notes

Use the analog stick for horizontal movement: a small push walks slowly, a full push moves at full speed. The stick does not trigger jumps. Double jump: release and press jump again in the air. Double-tap left/right within 14 ticks to dash in that direction, or use Q/E (back/forward) and the touch dash buttons. Dash works on the ground or once in the air per jump sequence; it grants no invulnerability and costs 18 stamina. Jump cancels a dash, and fast movement presses are buffered briefly.

Replacement (R): spend 25 chakra to escape hitstun, retreat and avoid direct hits briefly. Eight-second cooldown. It does not cure poison or burn. Pierce bypasses guard; it does not bypass Replacement. Poison and burn deal damage each second; weakening reduces outgoing damage by 30%. Reapplying the same effect refreshes its duration without stacking its strength. Status timers appear under HP.

Basic (J), equipped weapon (H), signatures (U/I) and bloodline (O) are separate attacks. Guard while grounded to reduce damage. Tap actions again after recovery; holding does not repeat. Specials spend chakra and have cooldowns. Blue technique timers mean cooldown; greyed actions mean insufficient chakra or no bloodline.

P2: left/right arrows move and double-tap dash, up arrow double jumps, N basic, B weapon, M guard, comma/period signatures, slash bloodline, V Replacement. On mobile, hold sideways and use the analog stick and action buttons. Open Controls to adjust opacity, choose a separate control deck below the fight or an overlay, drag and resize controls, or rebind keyboard keys; Save keeps the layout on this device. Separate layouts are kept for small, medium and large screens. P and Escape remain pause keys. One phone controls P1; choose the computer opponent. Changing modes starts a fresh loadout. Rematch lets you change characters and weapons.

TACTICS: Basics cost 12 stamina (16 for the third hit); weapons cost 16. Stop attacking for 0.75 seconds to begin recovering stamina. Block early for safety, or time Guard within five ticks of impact to parry non-piercing attacks. A parry opens a counterattack window and has a one-second cooldown. Sustained guard pressure breaks the guard meter; weapons and jutsus deplete it faster. Full pierce bypasses guard and parry. A clean hit during enemy attack recovery gains 20% damage. Blocked or missed melee attacks have extra recovery, so spacing and baiting matter.

COMBOS: tap Basic three times with rhythm; a ten-tick input buffer catches early taps. After a confirmed basic hit, weapon and signature inputs can cancel recovery. Guards, whiffs, Replacement and recovery gaps break a combo. Later hits scale down in damage.

AWAKENING: selected fighters fill a meter from actual HP lost. When ready, press T (P1), Y (P2), or the Awaken button while free to act. One activation per round. No healing or damage immunity is granted. The form expires after its timer; Kurama changes Naruto’s signatures while active.

ORIGINALS / TNR: Silent and Winta have sword basic chains. Silent: U Harmonious Slash, I Echoing Rift (pull and metal vulnerability), O Tetsugan stance. Guard during the stance to absorb 20% of chip, reflect 30% of guardable attack power and ready a sword counter (+35%, 1.5 seconds). Full pierce bypasses this guard. Winta: U Mizukumi dash strike, I Eclipse Drain (35% lifesteal on clean hits), O Shinrai Ōu (+15% lightning damage for five seconds). Proposed awakenings change signatures for ten seconds. These are real-time gameplay adaptations; the duo ultimate is a future concept.

Procedural prototype artwork. Combat effects and stats are gameplay adaptations. No online play or detailed anime sprite assets yet. The round buttons and thumbstick take inspiration from mobile action-game controls, while combat remains a side-view fighter.
